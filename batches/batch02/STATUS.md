# Batch02 component checkpoint

Seven new authored component designs pass independent source/export and visual review. Two book-stock arrangements reuse the same constituent volumes and are tracked as derived assemblies. All original-game adoption remains pending.

The curved collection, shelf/rib returns and upper canopy form a useful joined edge. Shared and single study pieces have different use and approach arrangements, and the simple stocked proof includes a supported reading surface and seat at normal height.

The three book-group arrangements reduce identical height patterns, but repetition remains noticeable. Further genre/palette/grouping and spacing decisions belong to eventual room placement and later catalogue expansion; this small batch is not an entire library's stock variety. Upper shelves are storage/display until a real local access plan is verified. Low shelves have measured support and browse-facing geometry, without claiming reach by every species or body.

Checks include actual exported triangle UV coverage, manifold/source reload, exact material bytes, 2mm structural seams, measured clear voids and133 supported stock/folio placements. Blender views verify component appearance only. They do not establish D12 integration, navigation, player/cart behavior, save compatibility, audio, in-game lighting or target-PC performance.

The independent review and exact reviewed files are in `qa/batch02/`. Freeze, compact delivery verification and durable backup precede further expansion.
