# Botanical edge findings

- A valid support graph does not require every plant in a grouping to share one visible rigid connector. Independently rooted clumps can be one authored planting composition; each needs its own real soil contact. An above-soil beam added only to force graph connectivity harms the intended organic form.
- A reused low coping profile cannot simply be extruded upward beside a retained seat. Its original small overlap can become an obstruction at sitting-body height. Keep the low bearing shoulder and seam, and move the rising coping behind the actual usable sitting space.
- A convex hull over a concave retaining section can fill the very soil cavity that the visible mesh leaves open. Split at genuine section breaks and test the entire declared cavity, with positive solid controls, rather than accepting a lower collider count alone.
- Rich editable source hierarchy and economical static runtime hierarchy serve different purposes. Keep the authored branches/leaves in Blender, consolidate only evaluated static export copies by material, and verify geometry, UVs, materials and markers remain equivalent.

The corrected source/export and joined component arrangement pass independent bounded review. Actual restore and durable backup remain the release gate; none of these component checks establishes engine or original-host acceptance.
