# Batch06: engineering utilities

Six original designs: AST-ENG-001 compact pump skid; AST-ENG-002 serviceable filter vessel; AST-ENG-003 wall control console; AST-ENG-004 flanged straight pipe; AST-ENG-005 compatible elbow; AST-ENG-006 manual isolation valve with a real wheel/grip axis. This adds technical purpose beyond furniture. No replacement room, fluid simulation, operating rating or engine implementation.

Inspect the actual technical blueprint and staff references. The blueprint's engineering/water-recycling/maintenance functions guide the family; its obsolete deck labels do not change current canon. Forms should show shaped supporting bases, service covers, mechanical connection hierarchy, accessible controls and useful maintenance clearances. Practical painted metal, zinc, grips, organized ports and restrained labels should differ from passenger resort furniture without becoming arbitrary grime or neon.

Metres, Blender Z up; ordinary asset front -Y unless explicitly stated. Source and GLB frames must be explicit from the start for all ports, mount points, valve pivots, grip/interaction positions and collision proposals. Follow standards/COORDINATE_CONTRACT.md. Actual exported-node/triangle checks with wrong-axis controls are required. Pose, opening centre and contact surface are distinct concepts.

The shared proposed pipe interface is standards/utility_port_spec.json: 0.16m bore, 0.20m pipe outside diameter, 0.32m flange, 0.035m flange thickness and six fasteners on a 0.26m circle. These are artistic modular dimensions, not certified engineering. Pipe author owns the new shared flange/port helper; machine author should reuse it. Shared mating profiles, closed supports and real unblocked bores are checked as geometry, not flow. Do not use fake caps to hide bad joins.

Use the existing staff-only material library unchanged. The console may add original readable local graphic textures, labelled static artwork rather than live telemetry. Preserve named moving/interaction parts when needed, consolidate ordinary static pieces by material role, and pack used textures. Editable .blend, generator, GLB, dimension/cost/contact/port records, source-to-GLB coordinate fields and conservative open-space-preserving collision proposals are required. No default boxes/cylinders passed off as final machinery; purpose must be visible in shaped casework, support and service relationships.

Before rendering, verify intended supports do not break through finished panels, including all repeated slopes or knees. Positive intersection alone is not sufficient. Normalize UV layers before joining; inspect actual GLBs. Use ordinary standing/use views at640x360 and targeted detail, with conservative camera preflight before heavy rendering. EEVEE_NEXT, no denoiser, heavy renders serialized by lead. A bounded neutral component assembly can demonstrate mating and access; it is not a new engine scene.

Independent source/export/visual/interoperability review and restored durable backup precede further expansion. Actual room fit, pressure/fluid behavior, device controls, actor usability, real load or safety standards, and target-PC performance remain pending.
