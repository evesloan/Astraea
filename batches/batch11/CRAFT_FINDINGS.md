# Landing component findings

These are bounded source-production lessons from the actual imported components. They do not establish original-host or game acceptance.

- A control face must communicate its service purpose in the image itself. The first staff panel's three plain colored squares needed an original SERVICE legend and distinct request/inspection/inactive symbols. Captions alone are insufficient; symbol semantics remain separate from working game actions.
- Exact coplanar tests do not catch every visible layer conflict. The guest directory's artwork was only 0.1mm ahead of a canted backing. It looked acceptable close up but striped at 640px distance. A real 5mm plate with the backing terminated at its rear face corrected the same-camera result. Do not repair such conflicts with lighting or render bias.
- Declared storage voids are necessary but not sufficient for good collision proposals. A waiting support box left the central opening clear yet occupied 234mm outside the shaped side. Section-following compounds retained useful profile space. Finite approximation measurements remain documented rather than claimed as complete physical bounds.
- Reused fixtures must be checked as complete objects. The staff housing retained its original 0.6075m drop and initially interfered with a proposed hatch. Lowering the hatch restored clearance without shrinking the fixture or changing the intended mount. Light aiming vectors and physical receiver normals remain distinct.
- A nominal reservation is not a host envelope. The R3 core/landing study guides relative placement and empty approach zones. No door, pocket, usable ceiling, support or shaft mechanism can be recovered from these component dimensions.

Successful unchanged views were retained with exact file and script provenance. Only the affected guest directory and staff symbol views were recaptured. The resulting four original designs remain separate from reused fixtures and from the proposed inspection arrangements.
