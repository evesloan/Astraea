# Batch05: aquatic leisure components

Six new original authored designs: AST-AQU-001 sculpted wet-area lounger; AST-AQU-002 companion low table; AST-AQU-003 straight coping/drain edge; AST-AQU-004 curved coping/drain edge; AST-AQU-005 modest entry-step/handrail assembly; AST-AQU-006 seating float with handles. A closed terminal recipe for coping may be a same-profile derivative, not another unique artistic design. No whole slide, new water physics, district rebuild or live game adoption.

Inspect the actual original waterpark reference before designing. Translate comfortable orange/ivory/teal character, rounded fitted supports, varied water/dry-edge relationships and clear useful surfaces. The earlier botanical diorama and residential references remain broader design context. This is a small reusable family, not a finished waterpark or universal species fixture.

Blender 4.3.2, metres, Z up, front -Y, standard Y-up GLB. Lounger/table use floor-origin Z0. Coping uses explicit deck-surface datum Z0 with construction beneath it, dry side -Y and water side +Y. Entry stair must document proposed level change, tread/rail geometry, dry arrival and connections; no safety-code, all-species or accessible-route certification. Float uses an explicit design center datum; intended waterline/seat/grip positions are proposals, not buoyancy or physics results.

Preserve all previous materials and assets. Use existing ivory_enamel/warm_stone/champagne_metal only where appropriate; lead supplies new aquatic-specific finish roles. Editable semantic parts, intentional shaped silhouettes and functional joints; no default torus/chair primitives presented as finished art. Use economical closed supports and real drained/slatted interfaces. No transparent water plane, emissive effects or new shader/pipeline needed for these assets.

Export evaluated geometry by shared material role, normalize active UV layer names before joins, retain any useful interaction/pivot subparts and explicit contact proposals. Source .blend, reproducible generator, actual GLBs, texture dependencies, dimensions, material/triangle counts, support/connection records and simplified open-space-preserving collision proposals are required. Physically impossible or floating support joins must be corrected before claiming component fit.

Use actual imported-GLB views at 640x360 plus useful detail; ordinary body-scale viewpoints and one composed dry/wet edge QA arrangement. All inspection lights/studio level geometry must be labelled as QA, not a game room. EEVEE_NEXT, no denoising, camera framing preflight. Heavy renders serialize through lead. Independent source/export/visual review and a full restored durable backup before any expansion. Actual engine integration, water simulation, species rigs, cart routes, saves and target-PC performance remain pending original project recovery.
