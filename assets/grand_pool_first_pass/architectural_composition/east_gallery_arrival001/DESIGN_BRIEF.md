# East lift arrival and Orchard Gallery rest bay

6 October 2026. Bounded source design approved by the parent after the retained-geometry screen. This is new source work only. No Game/Catalogue mutation, engine run or completed first-pass claim is made by this brief.

## The spatial idea

Turn the pale shaft into a recognizable two-level lift landmark, and give the upper east wall one substantial, inhabited rest bay. These are parts of the existing lift–stair–market route, not a new floor, shop or corridor. Keep the fore sun relief as the principal pool focal point.

The lift is functional retained architecture. Its shallow new skin must read as a fixed tower with directional wayfinding, never as a false doorway on its west face. A stepped, gently convex cream/terracotta profile grows from the lower wet level into a thinner upper portion and terminates at the real cap. The aft return faces the current long-view approach and points to the real east-facing gate. Retain the existing gate-side sign and all gate/cabin/door movement.

The upper bay is a real9m-long,3m-deep recess built in front of the retained wall. Use curved/tapered returns and a layered, swept canopy to make an intimate place off the generous gallery, rather than lining furniture against another rectangle. A curved built-in bench, two dense planted ends, a small useful table and an existing housed leisure display make the stop purposeful. Its front remains open, with a clear approach from the unchanged apron.

Its seated outlook is toward the opposite market edge and the tall shared volume. It is **not** a water-surface overlook: the exact gallery floor blocks the representative seated ray to the water. Place the retained media display on an angled side return within a comfortable seated view, showing existing original resort/ambient content. No blank screen or invented new shop identity.

## Shape, material and use requirements

- The upper canopy must shape the three-dimensional recess: a deeper warm-lined back, tapered/curved side returns and a lighter front soffit at a deliberately lower scale than the original tall roof. Avoid another giant flat header or a stack of thin trims.
- Keep all low furniture/planting inside the bay. Put the table off the principal bench approach, and keep planted ends out of media/market sightlines. Author standing and seated eyes, knee/foot bounds, cleaning-cart reach and a return approach with the layout.
- Use retained cream/warm-stone, aquatic terracotta, teal accents, walnut and champagne-metal roles. Metric UVs and aligned wood grain are required. No universal recolour, glowing border, untextured proxy in a claimed finish, or new material reconstruction.
- The shaft profile is deliberately secondary to the sun relief. Its geometry and clear directional information make it a useful landmark; it should not become a competing monument or fake lift entrance.
- Keep glass, moving gates, cassette pockets, sill/cabin travel, stair flights/guards, all original light exits and the entire pool footprint intact.
