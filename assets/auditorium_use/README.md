# Original auditorium audience and backstage use components

Two original editable Blender designs for the existing D11 cultural programme. The supplied theatre image informed the amber upholstery, finished timber edges and restrained warm metal; the public blueprint informed cultural purpose only. No reference pixels were copied. Both actual supplied images were inspected before authoring.

The original game is absent. These unplaced components add no room, seat allocation, audience capacity or staff count. The historical 2,200 theatre + six ×200 cinema seat pool stays 3,400. The adjacent-seat inspection pair is only a proposed geometry relationship. D11_service is a separate workshop allocation and proves no stage-wing connection.

The finite bore has a minimum radius of 20.2844 mm around a pin circumradius of 19.8 mm, providing at least 0.4844 mm radial running clearance through the whole rotation. The 24 mm retaining caps remain at least 1 mm clear of the pan’s minimum hinge radius; axial retaining faces stay in contact. This is physical geometry, not load certification. The optional adjacent-module datum is x+0.74 m, leaving 52 mm between the static repeated envelopes. Actual row pitch, numbering, rake contact and all species fit are unknown. No close-detail derivative is needed.
- **AST-CUL-004 — Backstage shielded cue and work-light stand.** Clipped supported base, hollow rear service column, enclosed proposed cable, broad work tray, original inactive rehearsal card, yoke and formed hood/diffuser. ## Source and exchange contract

Metres; source XYZ is Z-up and front −Y. Native GLB maps (x,y,z) → (x,z,−y). Each asset's floor origin lies between real floor-contact parts, rather than claiming a physical central support. Markers distinguish physical contact/normals, root pivots and nonphysical proposed datums. Moving markers belong to their actual movement root. Source/native world position extras refer to the true closed pose; source parent-local coordinates are separately identified. `marker_api.json` and each asset manifest retain the complete records.

Sources are editable semantic `.blend` files and a deterministic original generator. Export copies are evaluated and consolidated by ownership/material. Runtime mesh groups do not replace source semantics. Terminal exports are configurations of their design, not extra designs:

- `exports/AST-CUL-003.glb` and `AST-CUL-003_folded.glb`
- `exports/AST-CUL-004.glb`, `AST-CUL-004_aimed.glb` and `AST-CUL-004_service_open.glb`

The common palette is unchanged. Audience roles come from lead-owned `standards/auditorium_materials.py`; staff roles come from unchanged `standards/staff_materials.py`. The only local graphic is reproducibly drawn by `source/draw_markings.py` using original 5×7 glyph strokes. Rebuild with Python/Pillow for this graphic and Blender 4.3.2 for the generator. `dependency_manifest.json` includes all helper-loaded textures, including unused roles.
