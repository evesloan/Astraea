# Hospitality component interoperability fixture

Six final GLBs are imported onto a neutral QA floor: pantry counter, quarter-turn return, beverage station, dirty-dish return, clean plate/tray storage and a separate service trolley. This is a component arrangement, not a restaurant, operating appliance, recovered room or replacement player cart.

The counter and return share a continuous top/profile language and a measured 2 mm construction joint. Four samples test actual exported stone, closed bulkhead, walnut and ivory end faces. The beverage station uses the counter's published placement and all four support pads meet actual exported stone. Every floor-standing asset's lowest visual geometry meets the neutral floor.

The storage faces back toward the counter work side. The dirty return is separate from the beverage presentation. The trolley retains its own stationary push approach. Published body, reach, cup, tray, caddy and drawer reservations are compared against the other placed component mesh bounds; only the reservation owner's own geometry is excluded. Own compartments and motion are checked separately by independent source/export tests. No actor path, continuous collision, capacity, hygiene or service-flow certification follows from this layout.

The high guest overview shows the whole set. The ordinary guest view uses a 1.65 m eye. The original oblique work-side image remains preserved but is superseded as useful presentation evidence: it hid too much of the actual working faces. Two separate views in `../workside_followthrough/` now show the counter service side and stocked storage/trolley approach, with intentional close framing and unrelated components allowed to crop. Their exact current-source imports are recorded separately. Neutral inspection lighting is not a final dining or staff-area lighting treatment.

The service trolley has named castors and a physical grip, but no rolling or player controller. The dish-return caddy's extraction is a removable, hand-supported proposal, not a proof of self-support at full extension. The beverage graphic/control geometry does not implement dispensing.

The component scene, floor and camera setup are QA-only. All materials and dimensions are proposed assets for later placement. Engine integration, six-species use, real room/route fit, local lighting, runtime resources and performance require the recovered game. Stock cost and the fragmented initial curved-counter collision proposal remain explicit in standards/PERFORMANCE_FOLLOWUPS.md.
