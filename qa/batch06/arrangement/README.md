# Utility component mating fixture

Six actual final GLBs form a neutral inspection arrangement: pump skid, straight pipe, manual valve, filter vessel, elbow and a separately mounted wall console. The floor and small console backing are QA-only supports. There is no enclosing technical room, working fluid circuit, device logic or recovered game scene.

The five connected pipe/machine components use four coincident mating seams. Ten actual imported marker nodes are compared to source-space records; paired normals oppose, six-hole phases match, annular surface samples hit actual exported triangles and neck centres remain clear. These are geometric interfaces, not pressure, fluid-flow or structural certification.

Conservative maintenance prisms are tested against other placed component bounds. Own moving/service geometry is covered by the separate independent source/export review. The pump and filter lowest points meet the neutral floor; console mounting points meet the local backing datum. Human-scale control and access proposals require later species, actor and installed-route validation.

The high overview is a construction view. The 640-pixel standing view uses a 1.65 m eye and frames the entire assembly, so small markings are necessarily distant. The console approach deliberately crops the wider assembly and shows the individual control hierarchy at a normal standing eye.

The first placement put the QA console backing in front of the elbow in both broad views. Those images and their generating source remain in `../arrangement_initial_occluded_console/` as superseded presentation evidence. The corrected fixture moves only the console and its backing. Each broad camera now verifies a clear ray to the exposed elbow outlet against all other actual asset and QA-support triangles. Final appearance is still judged from the rendered pixels, not just that ray.

Current detailed flanges cost 2,328 triangles each. They are near-inspection candidates; a future economical repeated/distant export is explicitly open in `standards/PERFORMANCE_FOLLOWUPS.md`. No native game draw, memory, LOD or target-PC claim follows from these Blender renders.
