# Aquatic component interoperability fixture

All visible authored components are imported from final GLBs. The neutral grey platforms, receiving lips and individual bearing stands are inspection geometry only. The float rests on the lower display surface; no water plane, buoyancy or game room is constructed.

The fixture joins the compact entry to two straight shoulders and one curved edge using the actual closed-end derivatives. Their pads meet individual supports at the documented datums. The lounger faces the edge, with its companion table on one side and the opposite transfer proposal left clear.

The source-backed evidence includes five actual tread top rays, a clear central ray with a positive real-rail control, actual source/export hashes, support datum equality and conservative clear-space separation. It is component geometry evidence, not collision, route, accessibility, structural-load or species-contact certification.

The first preflight rejected a cropped overview before any heavy render. A wider lens corrected that framing; the initial log is preserved. Final overview and lower-level standing view frame all authored components. The dry-side entry view deliberately crops the nearby furniture and is labelled as a local entry view. The lower standing camera is 1.65 m above the lower display; the upper entry camera is 1.65 m above the upper platform. The high overview is a construction view.

All final views use the warm-stone edge palette and corrected entry stringers. Source-family historical views are retained separately with their earlier hashes. Real engineering volumes, boarding, water interaction and runtime performance remain pending original game recovery.
