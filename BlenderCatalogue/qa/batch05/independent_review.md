# Batch05 independent component review

**PASS for the six authored aquatic components and two same-profile coping closures.** Final source, export, material, connection, coordinate and visual checks are complete. This is a component catalogue review; the original game is unavailable and has not been reconstructed or executed.

## Reviewed scope

The actual supplied waterpark-reference pixels were inspected. The reference contributes warm pale contact surfaces, controlled orange/teal accents, rounded fitted supports, comfortable-looking lounge silhouettes and differentiated dry/wet-edge uses. It does not establish physical scale, safe construction, species fit or a finished resort layout.

The review independently reopened all eight saved Blender 4.3.2 sources without saving, parsed the actual GLB binary data, inspected assigned packed textures, checked semantic geometry and collision proposals, viewed current 640-pixel and detail exports, and reopened the final composed inspection fixture. Every inspected source stayed byte-identical during read-only reload.

Grip centre records are centreline poses; the mid-arch clear gap to the hull is approximately **121 mm**. Each handle joins its two saddles; the membrane/welt joins the coaming.

## Coordinates verified against actual exchange geometry

`independent_coordinate_report.json` reads actual native GLB POSITION/index bytes and composes the complete scene node hierarchy. It compares source records converted by **(X,Y,Z) → (X,Z,−Y)** with actual triangles; it does not infer success from source-space dimensions or rely on a Blender import reversing the conversion.

Eleven representative contact/pose records cover the aquatic lounger, float membrane/headrest/grips and coping mount, plus prior guest, library, staff and botanical assets. Correctly converted surface contacts agree within 0.00030 m; the largest difference is the prior bench’s nominal crown proposal. New measured membrane/headrest/mount positions are effectively exact at float precision. Converted normal directions agree. Every unchanged-XYZ negative control fails the appropriate surface/pose tolerance.

The prior staff cabinet’s actual articulated pivot resolves to glTF (−0.837, 0.2, 0.317) within 1.3e−8 m. Its four leaf/handle child nodes are retained. Applying the same signed −105° pose around the converted +Y axis to an actual child vertex agrees with the source-basis calculation within 2.0e−8 m; using native +Z produces a 1.18 m error. The current saved source and current GLB node extras explicitly name both source +Z and GLB +Y axes and both framed pivots. The unqualified legacy axis field is removed. The original files are archived; the compatibility history explains their old source-frame text. Independent comparison shows this authorized metadata revision leaves GLB binary geometry/image data and all non-extras JSON byte semantics unchanged.

Zero origins, proposed waterline positions, the open drain centre and grip centrelines are explicitly separated from physical contacts. These bounded checks do not validate every older annotation or implement an engine controller.

## Actual visual evidence and arrangement

Current raw and labelled views were inspected, including full 640-pixel silhouettes, ordinary 1.65 m camera views and contact/support details. Final render evidence hashes agree with current source/GLB files. Historical rejected images retain their original provenance.

The lounge’s shaped recline, the table’s useful split tray, the coping’s open slats, the entry’s clear route/returned rails, and the float’s raised cradle/recessed membrane are all readable. Final entry images no longer show stringer knees projecting through riser faces. Low-poly faceting at the float’s soft saddles is a modest detail limitation, not a blocking silhouette defect.
