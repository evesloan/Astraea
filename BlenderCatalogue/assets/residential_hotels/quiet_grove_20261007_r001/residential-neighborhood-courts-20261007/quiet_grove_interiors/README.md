## Hotel-specific architecture

The two base arrangements are staggered side-facing retreats and opposing offset longitudinal retreats. Each has left/right furnished compositions, and genuine two-rest and three-rest variants: eight recipes total. The third family rest position is adult-usable in the source design and does not presume a child or nursery. All 111 source adjacency edges use different compositions; this is a deterministic whole-house mapping rather than three samples.

Quiet Grove adds a protected dogleg acoustic entry, a low fitted entry soffit tied to the wet-room bearing, coat and drop storage, deep usable book/luggage shelves, separate resting approaches, acoustic shoulders and segmented retreat canopies. The inherited continuously rising main ceiling and curved perimeter remain. Window-side shared sitting and a separate reading ledge face the actual source receiving architecture: 43 outer garden/court outlooks and 40 inner planted courts. They are not called space or hull windows.

The retained enclosed bathroom and actual operable source privacy door, sliding source entry door and compact kitchenette remain separate real components. A source-only directional glass preview is clear inside and dark polished outside. Its unchanged sealed-r010 finite aperture has been numerically verified; this does not transfer the old runtime shader acceptance to these new variants.
