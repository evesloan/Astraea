## Architectural decisions

Family Terrace uses a broad8m arrival route, group tables in domestic canopy pockets, shared belongings shelves and a remote planted/acoustic quiet edge. Upper terrace outlines change in plan and depth, with a contained two-flight stair; there is no bridge grid or uniform balcony ring. The real taper and five original commons identities remain controlling. The inward room bank addresses its own enclosed garden with occupied reading edges, while the outer bank addresses the real large court across a private planted setback.

The new interiors have a real shared table and three-person seating, open divided storage, a broad arrival portal and a separate sectional living/sleep threshold. One plan gathers the party before three rear rest approaches; the other keeps the living bay beside parallel quiet retreats. These are new compositions, not renamed Tidal or Quiet Grove rooms. The finite shell, real enclosed bathroom, operable source entry/privacy doors, compact kitchenette and sealed r010 aperture engineering are reused. A and B sidewalls are selected and evaluated separately, and new joinery follows their actual profiles. Family means three full rest supports, without an assumed child/nursery role. No ordinary room was enlarged into VIP accommodation.

All suite glazing carries the source-only opaque polished/tinted exterior and clear interior directional requirement. The isolated r010 detail history, this source shader and static export do not certify full-house privacy. Exterior transparency, lights-on/oblique/upper/seated views and runtime material replacement remain adoption gates.
