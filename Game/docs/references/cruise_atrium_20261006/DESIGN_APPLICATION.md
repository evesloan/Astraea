# Cruise atrium and lift references, 6 October 2026

The owner supplied these two photographs as architectural references. Both actual images were inspected; original bytes and visible watermarks remain intact. They are inspiration, not pictures of the Astraea build and not dimensioned blueprint authority.

## Spatial language to carry into public architecture
- Visible faceted/cylindrical glass lift cars and clear vertical rails are integrated with substantial full-height piers, rather than detached utility boxes
- Curving balcony fronts and continuous gallery layers make the large central void legible, with occupied overlooks and views between decks
- Broad paired stairs, generous intermediate landings and clear arrivals join the social floors; the stairs belong to the composition
- Deep frames, cream/stone-like surfaces, warm metal and darker wood-like soffits give thickness and material contrast
- Supported hanging light volumes and warm integrated soffit/step lighting establish scale and welcome; translate these to restrained low-poly light forms, not literal crystal complexity
- The first reference includes a gridded vaulted glazed roof; the second a layered luminous ceiling. Neither authorizes removing an existing protected roof, changing pressure boundaries or exceeding the118m envelope

## Application and preservation
Use these references when authoring atrium-facing public lift lobbies, gallery fronts, grand stairs, theater reception and future nature/promenenade interfaces. Preserve Astraea's original design language, canonical deck datums, clear routes, core structure, stable lift identities and functioning controls. Any cabin/shaft geometry variation requires actual clearance and sweep checks; photographs do not certify dimensions or capacity. SunCorp staff facilities retain their intentionally more utilitarian character.

These references refine architecture while the complete ship platform remains the priority. They do not authorize new gameplay, species, capacity, track or room-allocation changes.
