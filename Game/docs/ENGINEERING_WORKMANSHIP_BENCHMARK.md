The engineering room is the user's established workmanship benchmark. Their October 6, 2026 review of its actual-game view was: “i still can't get over how good of a job you did here from this visual, the room and detail looks great.” The parent reaffirmed this direction on October 12 while the Group4, Group5 and Group6 integration package was being retained.

Apply its believable architecture, purposeful layers of equipment, coherent junctions and lived-in detail to future construction. Preserve each district's own identity, source family, protected allocations and operating interfaces. This is a workmanship standard, not permission to copy the engineering room's equipment or appearance into every district.

Future progress pictures must show clear player-height context and readable wall-to-ceiling connections. Identify source-only or studio pictures accurately; the user's approval of the engineering actual-game view does not establish final acceptance of other rooms, lighting, CRT presentation or target hardware.

Continue physical construction within supported interfaces. This direction does not request a broad new audit or change the retained tram, Upper Horizon, structural, occupied water, species, save or cross-owner interface holds. Main promotion remains parent-owned; the construction branch advances independently.
