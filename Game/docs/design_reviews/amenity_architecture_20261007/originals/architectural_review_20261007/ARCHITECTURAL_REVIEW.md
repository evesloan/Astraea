## Shipwide capacity and circulation gate for these four

A believable2,000-passenger ship does not require every room to hold2,000 people simultaneously. It requires explicit demand, distribution and service logic. Before architectural acceptance, record per destination:

- Intended activities and actual usable anchors by role; seated/resting/browsing/practicing/waiting and staff are different roles.
- Peak concurrent use and service rate from the ship's schedules/catchments, not an invented equal species or venue split. Separate normal occupancy, exceptional peak and total ship admission.
- Turnover and queue storage: sports session release, changing/rinse time, library dwell, garden quiet capacity, staff breaks and care downtime.
- Actual body and four-arm sweeps, family-group regroup space, companion access and supported poses. Six-species dimensions and full activity behavior remain unresolved; human furniture counts alone do not close them.
- Guest entry, step-free vertical route, exits/egress design, level loaded-cart maintenance route, clean stock delivery and dirty returns. Source packets have internal staff edges but no completed external service network. Verify connections to the existing staff-riser reservations and actual current owners.
- Meaningful views at arrival, first turn, seated height, raised edge and moving-cart height. Pair each with its true target volume; no implied exterior where a room or slab intervenes.

Test logical full-population operation separately from a bounded visible actor budget. Do not stage2,000 models for every screenshot or mistake an empty source render for populated acceptance. This is game spatial/operational validation, not maritime building-code certification.
