# SunCorp integrated signage direction — 2026-10-07

Owner instruction: all signage must meet the ship’s stylistic/design language, be SunCorp-themed, built in and intuitive. Digital treatment is an option. Oversized, tacky, blocky standalone signs are rejected for the luxury ship.

## Implementation direction
- Integrate guest wayfinding and information into architecture, counters, door surrounds and destination fixtures. Use restrained hierarchy, convincing mounting and existing district materials.
- Keep staff work labels compact, practical and built into their actual storage/work fixtures. Preserve clear product and safety information.
- Use SunCorp wording already established in the source. No new universal logo is approved by this direction.
- Digital panels may be used where purposeful; avoid indiscriminate emissive screens, scrolling clutter or added light-budget pressure.
- Verify legibility at ordinary eye height through the default CRT presentation; preserve existing RMB-focus/E information behavior and refit interaction targets to visible geometry.
- Preserve source originals, persistent IDs, routes and fixtures. Revise explicit source successors and verify untouched geometry rather than globally shrinking signs.

## First bounded pilot
Sports Care staff CareInfoPlaque/Stand/Title/Copy will be revised to a fitted shelf-fascia insert. Current revision is in isolated source development. No installed signage replacement or final visual acceptance is claimed.
