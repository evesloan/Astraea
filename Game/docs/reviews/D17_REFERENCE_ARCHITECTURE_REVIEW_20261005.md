## Five decisions to fix before construction

1. **Keep a compressed, deep commons threshold and release beyond it.** Preserve the aft portal's full 3 m width and 2.82 m clear height. Continue the existing wall/ceiling profile through a short reveal, then lift the new approach soffit toward the court within the ordinary 106.5 roof limit. This makes the existing commons the recognizable point of departure instead of adding a competing monumental gate. Keep rails, jambs and fixtures outside the clear aperture. Use an aft-only source derivative; the fore side remains physically closed.

2. **Make the court corner an overlook landing, with a real change of plan width.** Retain the narrow X78.5–81.5 approach through the protected central-view reservation, but flare the eastward junction on its aft side into a faceted fan-shaped landing in the available Z20–29 court band. A roughly 5–6 m local width is an architectural study target, not verified clearance. Carry the uninterrupted route along one edge and reserve a quieter stopping edge outside it. The fascia, guard and soffit should turn together; a painted floor stripe or tiny corner chamfer is insufficient. Keep the court visually open, revealing the commons and future residential frontages. Do not insert a second hero tree, bridge grid or full-height decorative support to fill the view.

3. **Recess Royalty's private threshold into its own allocation.** Keep the X91–94 lane legible and make its X94/Z29–32 entry turn into an approximately 3 m-deep arrival bay inside790, subject to source fit. Put the first privacy return beyond this bay so the entry reveals the lower gathering/view zone obliquely rather than a future bedroom or bathroom. Coordinate the recessed door, overhead compression and wall return as one threshold family. The lane must not become the suite's foyer, and the private bay must not consume the adjacent future unit or be claimed as separate staff access.

4. **Shape the lower platform around two different views.** Organize an inward court-facing arrival edge and an outward hull-facing gathering edge, joined by an offset, widening interior route. Use a meaningful faceted/sweeping outboard profile with a coordinated deep window reveal and overhead band; keep the main usable floor level at102. Let a smaller nested side bay provide the intimate counterpoint from8079. Do not simply clip four corners of the 24×22 m allocation. An actual external aperture and celestial sightline must be proved before promising the premium space view; inward glazing should frame the real court. Keep glass visibly present and reserve bathing/changing screening. No sunken pit or new opening into D16 is justified by these references.

5. **Reserve the private upper section without sacrificing the first lower room.** Draw one bounded inboard stair/private-lift/service zone within790 before finalizing the lower platform. Keep its future stair and lift approaches off the principal view axis, and keep the initial floor safely continuous until coordinated upper openings and guards exist. The upper datum remains106.5, roof≤112; this is the same home. A lower ceiling cannot claim the full4.5 m deck interval because slab, structure and services use part of it. A future double-height pocket, if selected, must be explicitly subtracted from790's own upper plan, never borrowed from a neighboring deck or inferred from the reference. Use a simple expressed floor edge/soffit and bounded frame/hanger solution tied to demonstrated bearings; preserve held bracing without treating it as a reason to continue hidden structure before the room exists.

## Material conflicts and release gate

- A uniform 3 m route plus a flat rectangular lower slab would meet a connectivity sketch but conflict with the required hierarchy and non-box architecture. Decisions 1–4 address this at the platform/section stage, before furniture.
- Copying the diorama's floor count, placing roof112 over the whole court, glazing through occupied neighbors, or removing the existing commons cover would conflict with the full liner. None is needed for this increment.
- Full-height supports at X84/Z24 remain rejected. Architectural appearance cannot justify consuming Night Garden or lower homes, nor can a believable-looking hanger substitute for an actual geometric bearing/contact check.
- The references inform original economical silhouettes, neutral pale panels, restrained warm timber/brass and local fixture light in the existing low-resolution renderer. Glossy photographic finishes, an unrelated shader stack or decorative density cannot stand in for the missing plan and section.

Before adoption, require a dimensioned plan and section showing these five decisions, exact existing-floor subtraction, and the chosen bounded support/ceiling interfaces. After root-coordinated integration, review ordinary-height views from the existing commons, the court landing and the private threshold, plus a section/exterior view locating this increment within the real ship.
