## Decision

Prefer a **bounded elevated station/guideway study at the fore-midship catchment**, rather than adopting a complete line. Hold X−2…+2 / Z−250…−182 for guideway-volume study, with running datum Y56.1, structural underside Y55.55 and clearance top Y60.1. These are explicit conservative architectural proposals: 4 m width, 4 m running clearance and 0.55 m construction depth. They are not canonical tram dimensions or a structural design. The retained pedestrian axis remains D09 Y52.5; overhead clearance would be 3.05 m, before signs, sprinklers, deflection and finishes. Require at least 3 m unobstructed pedestrian headroom in the next section review.

This 68 m segment does not encounter the shifted fore-midship core at Z−272. It provides a coherent place to resolve a genuine separate boarding level, maintenance envelope and accessible transfer before investing in the rest of the line. The elevated station is not installed and this study does not authorize extending into adjacent destinations. An at-grade station on the existing eight-metre route cannot automatically preserve four metres of clear pedestrian width after a four-metre guideway, segregation and boarding are added.

Propose a 24 m-long fore-midship boarding study at Z−228…−204. Guideway X−2…+2; port boarding platform X−5.5…−2.5, with the intervening 0.5 m held for platform/edge equipment. New level access/lift study X−7.5…−4.5 / Z−202…−198, connecting Y52.5 to Y56.1. The lift requires its own support/floor and upper headroom, a level upper landing and screened transfer to the platform. None exists today. Hold the connection corridor along the port side of this bounded volume; do not presume a usable bridge over a guideway. Platform length must be revisited against a real vehicle, swept body, door positions and recovery arrangements.

At a trial 1:12 gradient, 3.6 m rise requires 43.2 m of ramp run before landings, turns and handrails. A short decorative stair is not accessible boarding. A new local boarding lift plus stair is therefore the preferred study, subject to exact cabin, roof, emergency egress and transfer acceptance. The existing public lift stops at Y52.5 and Y61, not Y56.1. Existing wet-market and retail lifts must not be silently retimed or assigned extra stops.
