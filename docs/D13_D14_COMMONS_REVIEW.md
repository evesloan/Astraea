# D13/D14 starboard commons: implementation contract

Two existing commons programmes are being connected at floors84 and88.5. This is not a new accommodation or a recovered Moonwell identity. The historical Moonwell captures show D08/D09.

Independent constraint and circulation reviews found real interface defects in the first candidate: a1m gap at the atrium arrival, a blocked residential junction, narrow strips around stairs, mismatched landing width, unclear lift edge tolerances and proposed supports crossing the Night garden ceiling without coordination.

Revision2 removes the accidental strips by joining the stair opening to the main void. Both arrivals overlap the existing floor to X50 with a straight full-width throat. The upper landing reserves4.4m gross depth for4m clear travel. The residential guard requires a precise opening and finished returns. The rear lift strip is a shaft/service zone. Platform-edge and sill gaps are explicit.

Supports still require coordinated edits to the Night garden source, including fitted ceiling apertures/collars and regression checks. The new contract has passed geometric preconstruction assertions; these do not prove in-engine routes or structural loads.

The imported AST-DOR-003 gate is an editable Blender component with four telescoping leaves. Headless tests verify closed/open motion, fixed-pocket clearance, absence-of-cabin rejection and loose-prop obstruction. It is not yet a functioning installed lift. The controller must own its interlock, and actual loaded-cart transport remains a required test.

Next integration: coordinated bearing/ceiling interfaces, structural ownership compilation, stairs and guards, lift/cabin/button controller, loaded transport, then normal-height visual review. No changed guest counts, deck datums or original room identities.
