# Next whole-ship platform section

Reviewed 2026-10-05. Proposed next construction increment after current D17 regression closes: the fore-port D08 Grand Pool and D09 Orchard Market shared section. This is an implementation brief, not an adopted layout or finished destination.

## Why this section
Two currently unbuilt programmes can share an authored multi-deck arrival, pool volume and market overlook. Existing Gentle Waters and Orchard Exchange are separate destinations and do not fulfil these allocations. Do not replace the whole-ship goal with another isolated room or hidden structure exercise.

- Whole-ship planning and older programme-floor audit input hashes are stale. Reconcile selected current sources before using their coordinates or coverage totals as evidence

1. Resolve the complete public/service vertical section and ordinary accessible routes, including the raised wet deck; verify space for ramp/landings or an existing-family physical lift
2. Author the connected spatial shell, floor ownership, galleries, wet/dry boundaries, guards and real bearings together
3. Keep market perimeter usable and retain its retail-address relationships; decorative stock and swimming simulation remain later work
4. Test ordinary arrival/return, accessible level changes and loaded-cart service travel with actual walls and closed gates
