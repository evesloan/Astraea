# Axiom reference review for Astraea

Review date: 7 October 2026. Research only; no project edits, builds or new game renders.

## Conclusion

The useful direction is a linked interior town: broad transit streets lead between distinct open courts, while real building fronts, terraces and visible connecting routes give those voids purpose. Apply that spatial relationship to Astraea’s original ship, functions and low-poly CRT-dithered presentation. The flythrough establishes neither appropriate real dimensions nor functioning transit mechanics.

## Proposed translation to Astraea

1. **Linked tram streets.** Design the route, adjoining buildings and views as one section. Reserve the full vehicle/cart swept volume, boarding clearance, structural support and usable public edges first. Alternate narrower passages with arrival courts so a journey reveals destinations. These are architectural proposals, not permission to replace the protected route or change transit technology.

2. **Useful open volumes.** Give each court an identifiable activity, two or three legible destinations and a calm edge. Use the approach to frame its focal element. Preserve breathing room and choose crossings for genuine origin–destination pairs. The reference does not justify a bridge grid or one continuous empty megahall.

3. **Interior building fronts.** Build thresholds with depth, occupied floor plates, corners, soffits, window bays and a readable relationship between interior use and frontage. A shop should connect to its real addressed room and service approach. Avoid decorative façades hiding unchanged boxes or doors with no usable interior.

4. **Windows and terraces.** Windows can reveal a genuine internal court rather than space outside the hull. Coordinate the view, seating, lighting, glass profile and privacy together. Terraces need specific inhabitable pockets and functional routes, not repeated rings of railings. Keep glazing perceptible and do not expose technical or private spaces merely to maximize glass.

5. **Vertical circulation.** From the lower level, show where an upper destination is and how to reach it. Give stairs, escalators or lifts meaningful landings and clear connections. Model only the systems actually selected for Astraea and verify their operation; a visible capsule or ramp is not proof of usable inter-deck travel.

6. **Wayfinding.** Use building silhouette, route alignment, a view target, light and destination identity before adding text. Put clear signs at decisions and boarding points. The Axiom’s huge translucent advertisements are useful evidence of visual competition; Astraea’s low-resolution world should use restrained, original signs with strong contrast and a readable hierarchy.

7. **Grand arrivals.** Translate the sense of reveal, layered fronts and coordinated spectacle within the approved Arrivals section. Keep the integrated sealed fusion exhibit distinct from the botanical atrium’s focal sequence. Protect public entry, service access, the opaque D03 crew boundary and the separation of real floors from exhibit volume.
