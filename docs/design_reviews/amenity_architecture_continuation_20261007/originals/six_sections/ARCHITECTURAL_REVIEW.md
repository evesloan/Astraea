## Cross-section architectural judgment

### Purposeful section and occupied levels

Laundry and Depot have a reason to be single-level working halls. Their low task bays, higher handling streets and clear overhead services are sectional decisions supporting movement and use. A mezzanine would add circulation, support and supervision costs without an established purpose. Preserve level cart routes.

Night Table's two vaulted dining rooms and lower central service roof, Stillroom's low arrival/withdrawal/tea ceilings around a taller gathering chamber, and Team Lounge's two rising elliptical roofs already make genuine sectional decisions. Their lower roofs are not accessible terraces. Do not count them as occupied upper levels or add stairs to them to satisfy a visual checklist.

Cinema's occupied rake is an actual purposeful elevation change: viewing, tier access and return ramps explain it. Preserve that structure. Its 200 roles must not become a new gallery or compete with the Long Library above. The active shared receiver owner resolves bearing/slab/service interfaces; this report creates no competing solution.

The wider ship still needs linked inhabited terraces and destinations. For these six, that question belongs at the actual district approaches and adjacent owned spaces, rather than an invented terrace inside every room. None of the retained views proves an occupied external connector.

### Views and guest/staff hierarchy

All six current packages intentionally use opaque perimeter envelopes within occupied ship volumes. A lack of a hull window here is not a defect by itself. Preserve this honest containment. Internal room-to-room views and framed activity edges can make the public rooms worthwhile without a fake exterior scene. Reference REF-007/102/112 seascapes or Earth windows do not establish a real hull opening.

Guest/service distinction is convincing in the two D01 workplaces and in Team Lounge's cool support annex. Night Table and Cinema projection need the already-identified fixture/material contrast. Stillroom's support functions exist; its principal guest view can be improved by making gathering/tea the dominant end to the view rather than service headers and bare work openings.

### Functional capacity

Do not use total room area, shelf quantity, nominal chair counts, 0.65 m static route probes, or a small rendered crowd as interchangeable proof. The route/body/service evidence needed differs by room. Unknown operating capacity is unknown, not zero. No new admission, dining or crew-break capacity is created by this report.
