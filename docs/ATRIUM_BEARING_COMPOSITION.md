# Principal terrace bearings

The four main terraces now bring their eight profiled columns closer to the inhabited inner edge. This makes their distinct heights and structural rhythm legible across the atrium rather than burying them at the back of each terrace.

Moving the columns alone would have left their capitals terminating in the next gallery's opening. Each therefore receives a fitted branching fan which reaches outward to the actual upper gallery. Its three shoes meet the existing slab underside. The source has three height fits for the current 8.5, 6 and 4.5 metre deck intervals; these are variants of one design.

The inboard lens pair illuminates the terrace without being obstructed by the outboard fan. The center remains clear. These are edge structures, not cross-bridges, new floors or changes to accommodations.

Current checks verify 32 column supports, 96 upper-gallery contacts, 64 clear optical exits and a 4 metre wide/4 metre high reserved transit envelope on all four main bands. This is geometry and circulation evidence, not structural-load certification, actual cart handling or tram dynamics acceptance.

The terraces still need their local destinations, overhead finishes and furnishings. Visible supports do not establish completed architecture by themselves.

A normal eye-height D09 overlook is checked against the actual offset contour. Its sightline reaches the lower canopy through transparent guards; an earlier arrival-side position was too far behind the edge and the slab obstructed the view.
