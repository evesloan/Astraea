# Architectural profile checks before final captures

Use these checks for new joined structural, cove, seat and transition assets. They supplement the existing editable-source, coordinate and material contracts. They are component-production checks, not host-room, mechanical or engine certification.

1. Settle the plan and section relationship before fixing mating faces. An entry portal, quiet edge and planted edge need not form a flat row. Record the real proposed transforms, the historical evidence and all unknown host dimensions. Reserve only the intended overhead or infill region; never replace a hollow room or useful space with one enclosing collision hull.
2. Join the actual supported shapes. Check opposing normal and position witnesses against real evaluated source and native export surfaces. Keep surface normals distinct from optional light aiming or use-facing directions. A transformed marker that agrees with its metadata may still float away from its named physical surface.
3. Terminate finish layers deliberately. Before spending final-render time, inspect different-material faces that occupy the same visible plane. The advisory axial screen demonstrated in Batch10 can identify candidates; it does not prove visibility or cover every sloped surface. Keep legitimate opposite-facing butt joints and buried bearings. Correct exposed duplicate faces with real surface terminations or intentional reveals, not a lighting bias or hidden render flag.
4. Design collision proposals by structural purpose. Material bands, bevel rings and decorative strips do not each require another physical hull when an accurately justified structural compound covers their union. Preserve real openings, shelf mouths, under-seat spaces, curved clearances and component approaches. Record actual proposal counts and conservative approximation limits. Fewer shapes alone are not acceptance, and a source-level reduction is not measured runtime optimization.
5. Preflight actual imported geometry and context bounds before rendering. Preserve the original framing and sources when correcting a camera or placement. Ordinary standing/seated views, relevant seam views and a connected section each have different purposes; a detail crop is not a failed whole-object frame if explicitly labelled. Avoid recapturing unchanged assets merely because metadata changed.
6. Freeze sources for final pictures and record exact hashes before and after capture. Preserve failed and superseded evidence. A source-only rebuild must start without pre-existing new outputs or QA inputs and include every helper input opened, including unused loaded material roles. Current exported geometry, textures, interface records and review receipts must agree before packaging.

Unknown original-host door strokes, cassettes, head reservations, final polygons and floor support stay unknown. A successful proposed component arrangement cannot supply those missing facts. Final adoption still requires actual room, body/cart, door, save and runtime-performance checks when the original project is available.
