Astraea canonical species
User-authored replacement roster — 1 October 2026

Before you get too far ahead of yourself, update all of our documents and your memory to revise our alien species. Our new species will be as follows:

Koblins: Small goblinoids, pesky, troublesome and the cheapest of the bunch. Large families, and first time cruising space for most of them. No extraordinary needs

Mantelids: They are a sophisticated insectoid that largely keeps to themselves, enjoying the quiet of their cocoons. doesn't make much mess besides for their molting process which is extremely gruesome.

Humans: as expected

Baelins: Beluga like humanoids that have soft gelatinous cartilage instead of bones, produces gooey, oily trails that require special cleaners. Enjoys the baths, requires aquatic accomodations.

Munkos: An odd four armed humanoid that has an extreme appetite. Respectful enough, however.

Mindos: Artificially intelligent species that runs on synthetic hardware. Would be considered a robot by most, but they have recently received rights and acknowledgement as sentient.
