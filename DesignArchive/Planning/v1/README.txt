Astraea District Planning Study
02 October 2026

Purpose
A reversible whole-ship planning package for district relationships, true deck elevations, two proposed hotel-massing alternatives and a paired district-to-asset kit. It creates no game scene, new floor cuts or room reassignment. Planning and tailored component production can proceed together while exact integration remains gated on recovered geometry and review.

Files

editable/01..08_planning_sheet.svg: editable vector versions of the sheets
editable/Astraea_allocation_constraint_ledger.json: dimensions, proposed massing coordinates, assumptions and unresolved allocations
editable/Astraea_1462_ID_program_crosswalk.json: all surviving stable IDs with historical capacities; no proposed hotel assignments
build_planning_package.py: regenerates the graphics from the retained read-only baseline in inputs/

Interpretation
Solid geometry is a dated source baseline, not current-runtime verification. Dashed hotel boxes and connections are proposals or explicitly identified historical conversation relationships. The hotel boxes fit inside the known atlas plan footprint and maximum height range, but a detailed upper hull/roof profile has not survived in this package. No full room allocation or new local vertical core is approved.
