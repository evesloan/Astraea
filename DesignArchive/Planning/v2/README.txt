Astraea Preferred Hybrid District Study v2
02 October 2026

This is a reversible planning revision. No runtime, floor cuts, room moves, new capacity or current-project edits were made.

Preferred strategy
Three proposed hotel identities with four upper residential wings. The lower public programs remain shared destinations in their existing historical allocations. Whole historical guest allocations and complete forward/aft commons replace boundary-clipped rectangles as study references. These affiliation regions are not new walls, slab edges or final unit assignments.

Limits
All 1,462 IDs are retained. No room IDs are assigned to a new hotel. Complete historical rectangles are used for affiliation sketches because current room polygons and door/bed mappings are missing. Intersected rectangle counts are not hotel capacity. Only 327 of 880 historical upper guest geometry records are illustrated; remaining accommodations are unaffiliated. These are geometry records, not assigned units or capacity. The exact 81 luxury allocations, unit sizes, multistory floor relationships and upper-hull fit remain open. Proposed local guest and service cores have no assigned shafts or floor penetrations.
