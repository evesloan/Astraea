## One room, one construction language

The room should read as a companion salon: sheltered reading/conversation booths on the north side, an open planted social court to the south, a useful tea destination at its east end, and a framed return view toward the real botanical atrium. The existing public and service doors, 825.8 m² guest footprint, protected tram strip, deck levels and registry identities remain fixed for this pass.

The repeated section is an ivory structural return with a recessed blue-green field, a limited warm-wood inset, and a thin champagne arris. The return rises from a built-in seat/storage edge, bends through a cove, and continues into the ceiling. A lamp belongs to that constructed channel. There should be no separate tent-like roof, disconnected chair row or uniform green wall stripe.

## Palette and material roles

Neutral warm ivory is the principal architectural surface. Deep blue-green/navy belongs to recesses, selected upholstery and the main patterned carpet. Warm wood is reserved for tactile bench/book/serving surfaces and cove inserts. Champagne/brass identifies joints, handles and thin edges. Muted clay/plum makes seating varied without assigning a new theme to every object.

The floor receives a readable original botanical/solar pattern at a coherent world scale. Quiet booth rugs use lower contrast. Walls use vertical fields and framed insets, not one continuous horizontal colored belt. The retro view remains 640 × 360 with the established stable dither/CRT controls and separate crisp HUD.
