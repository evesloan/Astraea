# Astraea guest simulation

Species canon update, 1 October 2026: **Koblins, Mantelids, Humans, Baelins, Munkos and Mindos** replace the former roster. See [SPECIES_CANON.md](SPECIES_CANON.md) for exact traits and implementation boundaries. Runtime behavior below still describes legacy rig/profile indices until the six-species migration is verified.

## Implemented model

`GuestLife` (`scripts/guest_life.gd`) is a Node3D with 2,000 deterministic, persistent records. Setup requires the complete 1,000-accommodation registry and one real bed anchor per active passenger: 300 standard suites ×2 beds, 300 family suites ×3 beds, and 400 specialty habitats (300 single +100 double), totaling 2,000 passenger berths. The separate 400 crew berths do not create passenger records: they support a separate roster of 399 autonomous colleagues plus the player's own record. Every record has a unique ID, name, suite/bed key, fixed assigned bed, accommodation party/class, compatible suite family, palette, height, width, messiness, sociability, favored amenity and schedule offset. Every member of an accommodation party inherits that room's family/climate. Party scheduling and social destinations use the accommodation identity, not a two-person ID formula. Tidy and untidy individuals exist in every family. Each record preserves its home environmental tags and habitat profile (such as dry/humid, rest support, adjustable/dim light and isolation), and `accommodation_compatible` verifies that offered home support matches those requirements. These are explicit accommodation capabilities, not a numerical pressure/temperature/biology simulation; the game does not expose guests to harmful common-area distress.

The runtime currently has eight legacy prototype rigs assembled from original procedural forms. These are retired as a species roster, but their indices, cache keys and historical tests remain implementation evidence pending migration. Canonical species identity must be separate from rig and habitat profile. Existing articulation, meal-facing, supported sleep and body-size adaptation do not by themselves implement the six new species, Munko four-arm contact, Baelin soft-cartilage support or Mantelid cocoon/molt behavior. Mesh primitives remain prototype art, not finished production characters.

Legacy hunger, fatigue, social need and comfort persist numerically. Their shared biological interpretation must not be imposed on Mindos; synthetic power, diet and rest remain open. The new Munko extreme appetite also needs an explicit design rather than an invented multiplier. Hunger/fatigue rise with elapsed simulated time; replenishment occurs only while actually at a compatible successful activity. Meal need reduction requires a reserved real seat. Sleep occurs only at the assigned berth. Rest has fatigue hysteresis (enter above 0.90, recover to 0.55), avoiding repetitive commute/rest oscillation. Cleaning an incident modestly improves its source guest's comfort and permits a short thank-you response.

## Daily routine and travel

Individual windows are staggered by approximately plus/minus one hour, with a small suite-mate offset. The day includes assigned-bed sleep, three meals, a favored amenity, two possible social visits, quiet rest and travel. Needs may request extra food or restorative rest.

Every trip keeps a committed `destination_activity`, destination room, destination deck, target anchor, route, waypoint index and travel phase. New schedule intent cannot falsely change an old destination into a bed or restaurant. Dining intent entered during an existing commute is retained until it can actually be served, even after its nominal window closes. Cross-deck travel walks to the origin landing, spends explicit time in a lift, then walks from the destination landing to the activity anchor. A missing route leaves a waiting-for-route state instead of cutting directly through walls.

Travel uses `ship.route_between` and 3.2 metres per simulated minute. At the game's 0.6-minute/second clock this is 1.92 m/s. Ship transfer is abstract; there is no NPC elevator cabin. Long tram-served corridor legs can use abstract tram boarding, a brief platform wait, timed ride and alighting at the ship-authored tram stops. Walking detours are counted when choosing a useful ride. Whole-trip transit planning compares authored, screened access/egress at the two nearby stations at each endpoint, rather than requiring an uninterrupted straight corridor segment. A ride is chosen only when it saves at least eight simulated minutes over the full authored walking route. Passengers walk behind the ship-authored opaque boarding vestibule before becoming hidden riders, then emerge through the matching approach after alighting; arrivals are activated locally without rendering a high-speed body across the corridor. The abstract guest service does not board the physical player tram or simulate tram passenger capacity. Long journeys remain meaningful time costs across the public districts. The continuous-day test confirms every guest eats and sleeps; it does not assert that every guest makes every optional social appointment.

## Capacity-aware dining

The ship registry exposes each venue's physically modeled seats as `room.seats`; only an explicit meal capability or positive meal_capacity makes those seats dining seats. Theater/cinema seating cannot inflate restaurant capacity. The guest model does not hard-code a dining capacity or deck number. A reservation is unique by `room_id:seat-NNNN`, associated with one guest and preserved throughout travel and seated service. Venue choice weighs availability, distance, load and personal preference. A reservation is released after 18 simulated minutes of actual seated service; only then is the completed-meal count incremented. If every seat is reserved, the guest retains a request and waits near the venue entry, retrying at a bounded cadence. Requests are not silently canceled at the end of the dinner window. Generic amenity choices exclude dining rooms, so unreserved leisure visitors do not occupy meal seats.

Legacy damp and mineral rig profiles carry dining affinities; these are not assigned to canonical species by this document. In the existing model, every venue has a non-punitive adapted-seat fallback, with matching venue support represented as comfort rather than a harmful deprivation mechanic. A legacy low-body rig uses a raised booster/perch pose; per-seat or per-room height metadata is supported. Appropriate venue affinity gives a modest comfort benefit. Family-specific menus, food chemistry, detailed seat fitting, servers, ordering and animated eating utensils are not modeled. Legacy profile-based sleep accommodation is enforced separately; actual Baelin aquatic-home eligibility is a new hard requirement, not proven by old profile matching. Munko appetite quantity/cadence and Mindo consumption/rest remain unspecified. Waiting guests are coarse records; local visual selection prevents their abstract waiting cohorts from all rendering on one point.

## Social episodes

A conversation begins only when two available guests occupy the same room/deck within 3.2 metres. Both hold the same episode ID and reciprocal partner IDs. Conversations last 14–24 simulated minutes, improve social need and comfort, then record each participant's named last partner and completed conversation count. A newly arrived social visit gets a minimum 20-minute opportunity unless food or sleep takes priority. A scheduled conversation may stay in a sociable amenity the guest is already visiting, avoiding a cross-ship commute merely to speak to somebody. Cinema/theater seating and dedicated dining remain separate from that choice. Pairing is opportunistic, rather than a promise that every individual completes a conversation each day.

## Functional guest amenities

Shopping keeps the main amenity schedule but adds actual browse, movement between authored display anchors, inspection, purchase and parcel-carrying substates. Purchases use the room's original goods metadata, retain a bounded eight-item history and lifetime counter, and display a carried paper bag. Up to three carried parcels persist until the guest returns home. No player funds or real currency are touched. Purchases also queue a bounded soft staff restock request, coalescing repeated purchases into one shelf-service job per shop; on-duty colleagues resolve that background service and keep a completed-job count. This is deliberately small ambient commerce, not an economic stock/pricing simulation.

Aquatic visits walk in through a cleared dry entry and basin gap, follow a short loop at the actual water level, use a buoyant low/horizontal pose with paddling appendages, then leave via the same gap. Separate bath basins have separate loops; a swimmer does not float across the dry strip between them. Swimming time and visits persist. No floor-cleaning incident can be generated under water; damp footprints become eligible at the actual dry exit and stop after the guest has dried.

Theater/cinema visitors reserve one of the actual 3,400 numbered audience seats, walk through authored foyer/stair/gangway/row-front routes, face the stage or screen, and accumulate watching time only at their owned seat. The legacy broad-body profiles use block-end seats. New species envelopes and four-arm clearance require their own admission and pose checks. The two theater-gallery destination records reserve their corresponding tier in the same D11 theater pool, so gallery aliases and viewing bays never inflate capacity. A full compatible pool sends a guest to private rest and retries after ten minutes. Reservations remain unique during travel and watching, release on departure, and reconstruct from persistent records on restore. Real D11 theater records may project into the visible theater volume on D12–14 using canonical deck elevation and the shared actor budget, without entering off-deck interaction APIs. This is individual admission and watching, not tickets, synchronized show times, or verified full-load ingress/egress.

## Crew life

A separate 400-person roster has permanent real crew-bed assignments, including 399 autonomous colleagues and the player in the registered private cabin. The player record mirrors their presence and is never moved or rendered by the autonomous model. Colleagues have custodial, engineering, galley, care, gardening, transit, hospitality, stores, research, administration, command and training roles. Deterministic round-robin assignments cover laboratories, training/briefing, staff administration and command/navigation destinations. Duty starts vary within each of three shifts; short training visits provide at least eighteen arrived minutes unless meals/sleep take priority. These are harmless work/learning visits, not a clinical, navigation or operational engineering simulation.

Each crew cycle requests before-shift, mid-shift and after-shift meals. Meal intent persists across travel and service windows. Only eighteen actual minutes in a unique reserved D03 bench position completes a meal or replenishes hunger. The 300 real crew places, support heights, reservations and completion counters are separate from the 2,200 passenger places. Broad crew use bench ends; legacy low-body crew use the adapted booster pose. If compatible seats are full, crew wait at their own home berth and retry, rather than piling up at a doorway. This is a schedule with three meal requests, not a guarantee of three completed meals per calendar day; long ship travel still consumes time.

The two breakrooms provide fourteen registered physical places each, and crew recreation provides twelve, for forty shared break positions. Reservations cover both travel and use, with one colleague per position. Available nearby places are preferred; overflow rests at each colleague's permanent private berth. Chair/bench heights and facing come from the host registry. Eight-hour sleep windows and fatigue/work/break/training/meal totals persist. At most eight visible colleagues share the overall forty-actor budget, with a blue staff sash. Aggregate colleague cleaning and shelf service remain bounded; the model does not path a specific custodian to every offscreen spill.

Legacy saves keep IDs, berths and incident claims. Old crew schedules migrate to the new role/routine contract; old Universal-changing swimmers are moved to its real dry changing anchors and lose stale water movement. No ages, guardians, dependents or biological needs have been inferred from morphology or the word “family.” Dependent care needs an explicit contract for dependent IDs, consenting guardian/group membership, accompaniment requirements, allowed activities and adapted fixtures before a family-care routine can be added.

## Causal, finite cleaning work

Only a real arrived activity can originate an incident: a meal, an amenity visit, an actually paired social visit, an occasional staff work/break/training visit, or a small deterministic subset of completed thirty-minute home rest/sleep episodes. Home traces are considered at wake/departure at most once per occupant per day. Staff traces use a lower probability and a longer per-person cooldown, while preserving all shared incident limits. Each incident records a globally unique event ID, real persistent passenger or crew ID/name, room/deck, the activity position at creation, a separate reachable residue position, source activity, amount, time and optional conversation episode. Traces use the nearest explicit room `care_anchors` when supplied (such as auditorium side aisles), then `ship.cleaning_anchor_for_guest(g)` when available. Otherwise meal traces use a supplied per-seat `mess_anchors` location or an outward floor spill beside the chair, preserving exact `source_position` and `source_seat_key` for causality. Legacy meals produce crumbs or tea, and other traces vary by old rig profile and individual messiness. Canon now requires Baelin gooey oily trails with special cleaners and extremely gruesome Mantelid molting; event types, frequency, depiction and treatment are pending. These must retain actual source and claim identity when added. No cleaning meshes are created in this module.

Bounds:

- At most 96 unresolved ship incidents
- At most 10 promoted local incidents across deck changes
- A local `mess_created` emission at most once per eight real play seconds
- A ship incident creation cooldown of 1.8 real play seconds
- A 128-entry resolved-history ring
- Background crew handles one eligible incident per two real play seconds

New incidents are promoted only on the active deck within 28 metres of the player. Promotion is exactly once; streaming never re-emits an old ID. Background crew resolves unseen work after a grace period. Unclaimed emitted incidents may be crew-cleared when away or after a long local grace period, but only if the owner listens for `mess_resolved`, preventing orphaned visuals. `claim_mess` protects accepted/partially cleaned work indefinitely until player completion or explicit release. `acknowledge_mess` is idempotent: the first resolution returns true, subsequent calls false. Existing claimed work is preserved across shift catch-up.

## Bounded visual simulation

At most 40 nearby, non-colliding actors render (the `max_active_actors` setting can reduce the budget to 0–40) within 38 metres normally. While the player is inside the actual central atrium, the radius is 90 metres and real occupants of its other balcony levels may render at the canonical deck-elevation offset. Off-atrium rooms on other decks remain excluded. All levels share the same 40-actor budget and world-distance priority; the model records are never moved for rendering. Public `actors` contains only active-deck bodies for interactions and door triggering; `get_rendered_actor_count()` includes the separate balcony projection. Their records are the source of truth. Local actor records update each frame so short route corners are not interpolated across a slow model batch. Other records update in batches of 160 per frame, using their own elapsed-time stamps. Selection uses distance hysteresis and one-metre separation so abstract anchor cohorts do not render as a pile. Live 3D labels are off by default; the existing interaction prompt names nearby guests. Optional debug labels are limited to one aimed-at guest within three metres and a narrow camera cone. Actors scale in, animate their own silhouettes, and are discarded when out of range or a deck streams; guest identities are not discarded. No guest has a physics collider that can trap the cart.

## Integration API

- `setup(ship, renderer, player)`: initializes registry and deterministic 17:30 state
- `tick(delta, clock_minutes)`: call only while unpaused; monotonic minutes preferred, midnight UI wrap also accepted
- `set_active_deck(deck)`: rebuilds local projection without resetting records
- `current_guest_info(position)`: nearby name, activity, morphology, accommodation class/party, environmental needs, home compatibility, preference and greeting
- `accommodation_compatible(guest_id, room_id)`: verifies home morphology/environment support
- `get_summary()`: population/activity, event, conversation, dining and active/balcony actor counters
- `get_rendered_actor_count()`: total active-deck plus balcony visual bodies, bounded by the shared actor budget
- `configure_household_roles(party_id, guide_id, supported_id)`: configure fictional support roles within one family accommodation; `(-1, -1)` restores independent companions
- `claim_mess(id)` / `release_mess(id)`: protect or release accepted work
- `acknowledge_mess(id)`: resolve a completed player incident once
- `mess_created(event)` and `mess_resolved(id, by)`: owner creates/removes visual work
- `social_started(episode)`: optional telemetry
- `service_requested(event)`: optional shop-service telemetry, with a bounded internal 24-request queue
- `export_state()` / `import_state(data)`: save/load complete Variant-safe persistent state
- `advance_background(minutes)`: explicit fixed one-minute catch-up steps between shifts, without local chore promotion or guest reset

`ship.all_rooms` must provide accommodation IDs, family, deck, center, door and its actual beds; public rooms provide IDs, kind, name, deck, center, door and anchors. Dining rooms additionally provide seats (and optionally corresponding four-seat table centers for facing animation plus per-seat reachable `mess_anchors`). `ship.route_between(from,to,deck)`, `ship.get_lift_position(deck)`, `ship.get_tram_stops()` and `ship.deck_elevation(deck)` are required. Boarding uses `ship.tram_boarding_anchor(deck,stop,side)` when provided. `renderer` supplies the original lounge helper material/box/sphere/cylinder/shape/text3d APIs.

Save data preserves guest records, needs, assignments, routes, pending meal intent, reservations, conversations, event IDs, claims, clock, totals and crew history. On load, assignments and dining reservations are validated against the initialized registry. ConfigFile serialization retains identities exactly and round-trips floating values within tested numerical tolerance. Use explicit `advance_background` for large skips; ordinary `tick` is intended for continuously advancing play, not an instantaneous jump of many hours.

## Verification

Run from the project root:

```
XDG_CACHE_HOME=/tmp/meridian-godot/cache \
XDG_CONFIG_HOME=/tmp/meridian-godot/config \
XDG_DATA_HOME=/tmp/meridian-godot/data \
godot --headless --path . --script tests/guest_model_tests.gd
```

The deterministic test uses the actual ship registry and path implementation. It checks 2,000 unique compatible bed assignments across variable-size accommodation parties; 400 distinct crew assignments including the player, bounded crew actors, real shopping/restock progression, swimming at actual water height with no underwater cleaning jobs, quiet show-watching, all eight rig constructions and resting-body bounds against their real mattresses; the shared 40-actor cap and canonical atrium-balcony projection without model mutation or off-deck interactions; zero-delta pause behavior; genuine walking/tram travel and assigned-bed sleep; actual dining seats and unique reservations; all 2,000 completed dinners during 17:30→02:30; a separate continuous 09:00→next 09:00 cohort; numerical needs bounds; paired/co-located social IDs; finite incident bounds; multiple local event cadence/deduplication; background crew; protection of claimed work; idempotent player completion; and ConfigFile round-trip persistence. Rendered art and collision-route QA remain separate from this headless model test and are owned by the integrated build review.

### Standalone rendered pose gallery

With a real display, run `godot --path . --script tests/guest_pose_gallery.gd`. ### Crew and audience remediation regression

Run `godot --headless --path . --script tests/crew_audience_regression.gd` with the same XDG directories. This uses the production registry and lifecycle records. It checks unique physical break places/private-home overflow at 17:30; all specialist work destinations; all 3,400 actual seat-route endpoints; saturated 200-seat cinema admission, release and reuse; current save round-trips; legacy dry-changing migration; and a continuous 48-hour crew pass with owned compatible bench seats, meals, actual sleep, arrived training and claimed/idempotent cabin care. It preserves the original separate 2,000-passenger dinner/day/save regression. Seat-route endpoints and logical capacity are not a physical collision, evacuation or full-audience test. These earlier assertions do not cover the newer supported-party and facility-admission work. Independently derived habitat eligibility and exactly three completed meals per calendar day remain outside these assertions.

### Interior-profile save migration

A room may retain its stable ID and accommodation envelope while its true world-X/Z floor contour changes. On import, `interior_profile.boundary` is authoritative. Old occupants outside that contour move to the nearest current authored indoor anchor; their identity, home, needs, activity time, visits and schedule intent are retained. Incoming targets and obsolete remaining indoor waypoints are repaired through the current ship route API. The remaining time of an active lift is preserved; an active tram retains its occupied leg and elapsed ride while its obsolete suffix is replanned. Current valid saves and a second import of a migrated save remain unchanged.

Unresolved incidents in the changed room retain event IDs, claims, progress, emission/status fields, amounts, timestamps and their historical `source_position`. Only an obsolete usable residue position moves to a current care anchor. Import emits no event/completion signal and creates no service or completion credit. The host's incident-display restore must read the migrated lifecycle position while retaining its separate partial dirt and career/payment fields. No layout-signature expansion or whole-career/population reset is used.

`tests/profile_import_regression.gd` performs a ConfigFile round-trip with a stale arrived salon occupant, a real salon colleague, incoming/outgoing walkers, active lift/tram travel, obsolete interior waypoints and an emitted, partially completed, claimed event. It checks preserved identity/home/needs/schedule state, real route endpoints, current-profile no-op import, migration idempotence, zero duplicate promotion and exactly one resolution/completion credit. Host player/cart placement, incident visuals, partial-dirt UI and monetary state are covered by the integrated host review, not this model test.
