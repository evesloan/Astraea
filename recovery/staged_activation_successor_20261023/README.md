## Activation and limits

ASTRAEA_STAGED_ROOM_ACTIVATION=1 opts in, with ASTRAEA_ROOM_ACTIVATION_QUEUE=1 and ASTRAEA_PACKED_CONTACT_FACES=1. Private staging additionally requires ASTRAEA_PRIVATE_CONTACT_SINGLE_PASS=1. All remain independently selectable and off by default. Ordinary deferred received homes use the stage path; Group2 cached/controller homes do not. Base private details and Night Garden manager records that actually delegate to the base are eligible; Night Garden's special partition loader and D04 stay on their existing paths. The fixture exercises two representative retained prototypes, not certification of all eligible rooms.

There is one active job across both managers: one resource request/scene or orphan visual, with resident-lifetime strong references, and the existing deduplicated visibility queue. ResourceLoader threaded get is called only after loaded status; resource loading is distinct from main-thread instantiation and SceneTree commit. Cancelled requests are drained before starting another job. No worker mutates SceneTree, no global shape/room pool and no retention of departed visuals are added.

Preparation stages: request/wait; instantiate off-tree; bounded mesh configuration and existing texture interner prewarming; bounded transformed face accumulation; native shape creation; collider attachment; one completed visual commit. The existing weak collision cache/key, exact triangles, glass contacts, policies, shell removal, identities and margins are preserved. Prepared colliders remain outside the SceneTree until complete. Configuration and face loops yield after the soft budget; individual mesh/material operations, scene instantiation, native shape/BVH work and final commit cannot be preempted. These overruns are explicitly reported, not hidden by the scheduler.

The near-distance and explicit prepare_position paths are unchanged and synchronous. A safety arrival can temporarily coexist with one prepared orphan; the job detects that the real resident now exists and disposes its superseded orphan. This preserves collision readiness, but does NOT promise elimination of all transient resource peaks. The final fixture tests this overlap and equal final geometry. Whole-world teardown during an in-flight threaded resource request is not covered by this fixture; the supported cancellation checks keep the scheduler alive to drain requests.
